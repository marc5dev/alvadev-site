Legal

Delete your data

You can have everything we hold about you removed, including anything from a WhatsApp or Facebook conversation.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

In one line

Email hello@alvadev.com with the subject Data deletion, or reply DELETE in our WhatsApp chat. Everything is gone within 30 days.

By email

  1. Write to hello@alvadev.com with the subject Data deletion.
  2. Tell us the identifier we know you by — the phone number you used on WhatsApp, the email address you wrote from, or your Facebook profile name. Without it we cannot find the right record.

On WhatsApp

Reply DELETE in the same chat. That is enough — the number the message comes from tells us which conversation to remove. Replying STOP instead keeps the conversation but ends any further messages from us.

What happens next

We confirm that we received the request and delete the data within 30 days. That covers the message history, your phone number or email address, and anything derived from them in our systems.

The one exception is data we are legally required to keep — invoices and related records under tax law, for example. Those are blocked from any further use and deleted once the retention period ends. If any of your data falls into that category, we will say so in our reply rather than leave you guessing.

What we cannot delete for you

Meta keeps its own copy of what passes through WhatsApp and Facebook, and we have no access to it. To deal with that, use the privacy settings inside the WhatsApp or Facebook app, or contact Meta directly.

Related

The privacy policy explains what we hold and why, and lists your other rights under the GDPR — access, correction, restriction, portability and objection.
