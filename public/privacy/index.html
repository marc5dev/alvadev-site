Privacy policy

What happens to your data when you visit alvadev.com or get in touch. The short answer is: remarkably little.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

The short version

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts.
  • The site keeps exactly one thing in your browser — whether you switched the background animation off. It never leaves your device.
  • If you email or message us, we keep that conversation until it is dealt with, and then no longer.

Visiting this website

The site is served by Cloudflare, Inc. (EU representative: Cloudflare Germany GmbH, Rosental 7, 80331 Munich) from its edge network. To deliver a page and to defend against attacks, Cloudflare processes the technical data every web request carries: your IP address, the time, the URL requested, the HTTP status, the amount of data transferred, the referring page and your browser identification.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in a website that works and is not trivially attackable. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Cloudflare. These records are short-lived and are not combined with anything else or used to build a profile.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts, which is why you will not find a cookie banner.

It does write one entry to your browser's local storage, called alvadev:aurora, and only if you switch the animated background off — so it stays off next time. It holds the character 0 or 1, is readable only by this site, is never transmitted anywhere, and clearing your browser data removes it. Since it exists purely to carry out a setting you asked for, no consent is required for it.

Getting in touch by email

Write to us and we process what you send — your address, your name and the content of the message — in order to answer. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the exchange concerns a contract or steps leading to one, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We delete correspondence once it is settled and no statutory retention period applies.

WhatsApp Business

We can be reached on WhatsApp, using the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd., Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland. If you message us there, we process your phone number, the profile name you have set on WhatsApp, and the content of your messages, in order to reply.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for contract-related exchanges, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in replying on the channel you chose. Messages we send you first are sent only with your prior explicit consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw that consent at any time, with effect for the future, by replying STOP on WhatsApp or emailing hello@alvadev.com.

Meta processes metadata about the conversation on its own account and may transfer data to third countries, in particular the United States. Meta Platforms, Inc. is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, and standard contractual clauses apply in addition. We have no influence over Meta's own processing — see the WhatsApp Business Data Transfer Addendum.

If you would rather not use WhatsApp, email reaches us just as well: hello@alvadev.com.

Facebook page

We operate a business page on Facebook. When you visit it, Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. processes personal data and produces anonymised statistics from it ("Page Insights"). For that processing we are a joint controller together with Meta under Art. 26 GDPR; the essential terms are set out in Meta's Page Insights addendum, in which Meta takes on primary responsibility for your rights regarding Insights data. Visiting our website does not involve Facebook in any way — there is no like button, pixel or embedded content here.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you (Art. 15);
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16);
  • have your data erased (Art. 17) — see Data deletion;
  • have processing restricted (Art. 18);
  • receive your data in a portable form (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21);
  • withdraw any consent you gave, at any time, with effect for the future.

One email to hello@alvadev.com is enough to exercise any of them.
